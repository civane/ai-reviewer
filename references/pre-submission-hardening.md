# Pre-submission hardening

Use this before a paper is submitted or revised. It is an evidence audit, not a request to invent hostile objections.

- Define every symbol before first use; keep notation identical across text, equations, algorithms, and appendix.
- Check dimensions, indices, conditioning variables, sign conventions, normalizations, and edge cases.
- Match theorem assumptions and quantifiers to practical regime; identify proof gaps and scope limits.
- Reconcile headline numbers with metrics, splits, seeds, hardware, and comparison budgets.
- Check captions, units, uncertainty, baseline parity, ablations, and qualitative selection.
- Make abstract, introduction, conclusion, and captions no broader than the evidence; add caveats where necessary.
