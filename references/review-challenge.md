# Review challenge and AC brief

Use this mode to make a contested review legible to a decision maker. The objective is to separate decision-relevant flaws from misunderstandings or unsupported assertions—not to pressure a reviewer.

| Classification | Required handling |
| --- | --- |
| Valid | Acknowledge it; state the repair or narrowed claim. |
| Partly valid | Identify the exact valid boundary and the residual fix. |
| Not supported by supplied record | Quote the concern; state what evidence would be needed before treating it as a flaw. |
| Factually contradicted | Quote the concern and cite the exact contrary page/table/equation/code result; give the shortest correct reasoning. |
| Outside stated claim | Quote the claim boundary and explain why the requested evidence is not needed for that claim. |

Call a concern factually contradicted only when the record contains a direct, checkable conflict. Do not make character claims, score demands, tool-use speculation, or rhetorical attacks. End with what the record supports, what it does not, and the most material remaining issue.
